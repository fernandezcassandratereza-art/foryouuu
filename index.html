a letter i kept close

there are things i never got to say...

to: mahal♡

Dear Mahal,

Honestly, I don’t even know how to start this because saying goodbye to you is harder than I thought. I keep trying to find the right words, but none of them feel enough. So I’ll just say what I can, even if it hurts a little.

I know things won’t always stay the same, and maybe this is really where we have to let go. But please don’t think that what we had was just nothing to me. I cared about you, more than I probably showed sometimes. I’ll miss the random talks, the little updates, the laughs, and even the simplest moments that I didn’t know I would end up missing this much.

I wish things could have stayed a little longer, but I also know I can’t keep holding on to something that’s meant to change. I hope life treats you well, Mahal. I hope you reach the things you’ve been wishing for, meet people who make you happy, and never lose the good parts of yourself. Even if I’m no longer there to see it, I’ll still be wishing you the best.

please take care of yourself, okay?
